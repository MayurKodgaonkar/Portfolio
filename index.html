The Business Problem
The regional analytics function at Asian Paints ran on manual, fragmented processes — consuming 4 hours of analyst time every day just to consolidate data before any real analysis could begin. Two systems, SAP SD and Salesforce CRM, had never been unified. No one could answer whether a field executive's visits were converting into orders or collections. Dealer payment behaviour was tracked anecdotally. Receivables aging had no risk classification. Leadership was making commercial decisions on stale, manually-assembled data with no systematic early-warning mechanism for credit risk.

Ingestion Layer Power Query automated ETL replaced all manual SAP/Salesforce extraction. Single-click refresh reduced 4-hour daily prep to under 10 minutes.
Star Schema Model Sales Invoices as central fact table. Dimensions: Geography, Customer, Product, Time, Sales Executive. Memory-optimised for 500K+ row performance.
DAX Intelligence Dealer PEI (Payment Efficiency Index), overdue aging buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), field ROI correlation, and MoM/YoY variance sliced by 4 dimensions.

Analyst Capacity: ~1,000 hours of annual analyst time reclaimed from data preparation. Redirected into actual analytical work: pricing analysis, promotional scheme effectiveness, and customer segmentation studies.

The Business Problem
A new, high-capacity warehouse launched with no historical data, no benchmarks, and zero analytics infrastructure. Leadership needed it to be data-driven from Day 1 of operations — not weeks later once data had accumulated. A structural SAP constraint compounded the challenge: SAP stores only current stock levels, making historical inventory analysis technically impossible without custom engineering. The entire platform — ingestion, transformation, modelling, and dashboards — had to be delivered within a 14-day compressed window, with warehouse operations commencing simultaneously.
Solution Architecture — Medallion Framework
Bronze

Gold

The SAP Stock Gap — Engineering Solution SAP exposes only current stock. Built a custom PySpark snapshotting algorithm that back-calculates daily historical stock by reconciling current inventory against historical Sales Orders and Inbound receipts — reconstructing a full inventory timeline from transactional audit trails where none existed.
Day 1 Benchmarking By back-calculating historical stock data, the new facility could be compared against legacy warehouse baselines from the first day of operation — turning a data disadvantage into a structured improvement roadmap with specific, measurable gap targets.

Decision Outcomes
Serviceable vs. Total OFR: Leadership could distinguish stock-out failures from processing delays for the first time — enabling targeted corrective action on each cause independently rather than treating all fulfilment failures as one undifferentiated problem.
Benchmarking as a Roadmap: ICT and TAT comparison against legacy warehouses turned the new facility's data disadvantage into a structured improvement roadmap. Specific, measurable gap targets replaced generic "improve operations" mandates.
Dead Stock Recovery: Slow-moving inventory identified within Month 1 through ABC velocity analysis, triggering targeted liquidation campaigns that would otherwise have taken quarters to surface through manual stock reviews.

The Business Problem
Senior Leadership and Finance faced a fundamental blind spot: no one could reliably state what the actual margin on a product was, after all discounts and costs. The structural complexity was severe — Finolex operates an 8-level successive discount cascade (Gross Sale through eight sequential deduction tiers to final Sales Realization), requiring joins across SAP SD invoices, Bill of Materials costing, GL cost centers, and custom scheme Z-tables. None of this existed in automated form. Finance produced profitability estimates through fragmented, analyst-dependent spreadsheets that were inconsistent, time-consuming, and impossible to slice by geography or salesperson at scale. Every pricing discussion started with disputed margin figures.
Solution Architecture — Medallion Framework
Bronze
4 SAP modules + 3 custom Z-tables · ADF incremental pipelines · full lineage in ADLS
Silver
8-tier discount cascade (iterative PySpark) · BOM joins · validation checkpoints at each tier
Gold
Profitability Star Schema · per-kg conversion · dual-consumption design
Consumption
Power BI (visual) + Excel Power Pivot (Finance self-serve) — same Gold layer
The 8-Tier Engineering Challenge Each discount tier's base is the net value after the prior tier's deduction — a cascade that fails catastrophically if any level is processed out of order. Implemented iterative PySpark with validation checkpoints at each tier (D1→D8), outputting an intermediate auditable dataset per tier. Finance can reconcile any level independently.
Dual Consumption Design Power BI for leadership visual analytics and Excel Power Pivot for Finance ad-hoc modelling — both connected to the same Gold layer. Finance analysts can run sensitivity scenarios without raising data requests, eliminating a chronic bottleneck between Finance and the data team.
Price Pipeline — Margin Waterfall

Illustrative cascade: each bar represents the cumulative deduction at that stage. The amber bar is the final Contribution Margin — the true metric that informs pricing decisions.

KPI Framework — Margin Metrics
KPI Definition & Business Use Analysis Dimension
Gross Sales Total invoiced value before any deductions — the starting point of the cascade Geo · Product · Salesperson
Total Discount Applied Cumulative sum across all 8 successive discount tiers (D1–D8) Per tier breakdown
Scheme Discount Discount attributable specifically to ZSCHEMES entries — isolates trade promotion spend Scheme · Channel · Period
Net Sales Gross after all discount tiers — the revenue figure Finance uses for reporting Per-kg conversion
Material Cost (BOM) BOM-derived raw material cost per finished unit from MAST/STKO/STPO tables Product Group · Material
Contribution Margin Net Sales minus Material & Conversion Cost — the true decision metric for pricing Full dimensional slice
Sales Realization Final revenue actually realised vs. original Gross — the ultimate efficiency measure Salesperson · Region
Strategic Business Value
Pricing Decisions on Fact: Leadership can now ground pricing strategy in auditable, automated per-kg margin data by Geography, Material Group, and Salesperson — replacing approximated spreadsheet estimates that varied analyst-to-analyst and generated disputed margin figures in every pricing discussion.
Trade Promotion ROI: Isolating scheme discount contribution (ZSCHEMES) from standard pricing discounts lets the commercial team evaluate whether trade promotion spend generates proportionate revenue uplift — a question that had no systematic answer before this platform.
Finance Self-Service: Dual-consumption design (Power BI + Excel Power Pivot on the same Gold layer) enables Finance analysts to run sensitivity models independently, without raising data requests for every what-if scenario, eliminating a chronic dependency bottleneck.
